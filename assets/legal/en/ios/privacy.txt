2.3. If the user is located in a jurisdiction whose laws grant additional mandatory rights, the Company shall comply with such requirements to the extent that they apply to the Company and the relevant processing activities.
3. Core Processing Principles
The company processes personal data based on the following principles:
data is collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and is not processed in a manner incompatible with those purposes;
the volume of data is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the operation of the relevant function;
where possible, on-device processing, aggregation, pseudonymization, and other risk mitigation measures are applied;
iOS permissions are requested immediately before the corresponding feature is used and are accompanied by a clear explanation;
Declining an optional permission, including a tracking permission, must not deprive the user of access to core functionality that is objectively independent of such permission;
Personal data is never sold or shared with data brokers.

8. App Tracking Transparency, Advertising, and Identifiers
8.1. "Tracking" within the meaning of Apple's guidelines refers, in particular, to linking data collected about a user or device within the App with third-party data for the purposes of targeted advertising or advertising measurement, as well as to sharing such data with a data broker.
8.2. If the Application engages in tracking, the Company shall use the AppTrackingTransparency framework and obtain the user's system permission prior to initiating any tracking. Before permission is granted, the Company shall not access a non-zero IDFA value or employ alternative methods, including fingerprinting, to circumvent the user's choice.
8.3. Opting out of tracking does not restrict access to the App's core functionality and does not result in less favorable conditions, except for the absence of personalized advertising or features that are objectively dependent on permitted tracking.
8.4. Users may change their tracking permission in iOS settings. The Company also provides a mechanism for withdrawing marketing consent where applicable.
8.5. If personalized advertising and tracking are not used in the current version of the App, the ATT prompt must not be displayed, the IDFA must not be collected, and the App Privacy Details must accurately reflect the actual absence of tracking.

9.3. The SDK provider may not use Application data for its own incompatible purposes, tracking, or profiling unless this is disclosed to the user, reflected in the App Privacy Details, and based on valid consent and a legal basis.

14. Account and Data Deletion
14.1. If the App allows users to create an account, it must provide users with the ability to initiate account deletion directly within the App, in accordance with Apple's requirements.
14.2. Account deletion means removing the account from active systems and deleting or irreversibly anonymizing any associated data that the Company is not required to retain. Simply deactivating, logging out of, or uninstalling the Application from a device does not constitute account deletion.
14.3. To prevent accidental or fraudulent deletion, the Company may request reasonable identity verification and clearly communicate the steps involved and the expected outcomes. Unreasonable barriers, including mandatory phone calls, shall not apply if the account was created through the Application without a comparable requirement.
14.4. Certain information may be retained in restricted access after account deletion where required for tax and accounting purposes, fraud prevention, law enforcement, dispute resolution, protection of legal claims, or safety purposes. Such data is not used for marketing or profile reinstatement.
14.5. Deleting your Asteria account does not necessarily cancel your Apple subscription. The subscription must be cancelled separately in your Apple ID/App Store settings if it is still active.
15. User Rights
Depending on the circumstances, the user has the following rights:
the right to obtain information about the processing and a copy of personal data;
the right to correct inaccurate or complete incomplete data;
the right to request the deletion of data ("right to erasure") where there are no grounds for its further retention;
the right to restrict processing;
the right to receive the provided data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller, where applicable;
the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to unconditionally object to direct marketing;
the right to withdraw consent at any time;
the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects, where applicable;
the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and to seek redress through the courts.
15.2. The Company typically responds without undue delay and no later than one month after receiving a request. The deadline may be extended in cases provided for by law, of which the user will be notified.
15.3. The Company may request information necessary to verify identity but must not collect excessive data. Requests are generally fulfilled free of charge; a fee may be charged, or a request refused, only in cases permitted by law, such as where requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive in their repetition.
16. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling
16.1. Asteria generates personalized content based on data provided by the user, but does not use it to make decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significantly impact the user.
16.2. Astrological calculation results must not be used as the sole basis for decisions regarding health, finances, employment, education, insurance, legal matters, or safety.

17.2. If the Company becomes aware that it has received a child's data without a proper legal basis or the required consent of a person with parental responsibility, it will take reasonable steps to delete that data and close the account, unless retaining it is necessary to protect the child or comply with applicable law.
17.3. If the Application is in the future intended for or likely to be accessible to children, the Company shall, prior to launching the relevant feature, conduct a separate risk assessment and apply the requirements of the UK Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code), including high privacy settings by default, minimization of geolocation and profiling, and clear age-appropriate notices.

18.3. In the event of a security breach, the Company assesses the risk to the rights and freedoms of users and, where required by UK GDPR, notifies the ICO and affected users within the prescribed timeframes.
